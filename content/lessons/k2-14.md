## Point
### 一般角と弧度法
VI: Góc lượng giác và đơn vị radian
EN: General angles and radian measure

動径 OP が始線 OX から回転した角を **一般角** といい、\(\theta+360^\circ\times n\)（\(n\) は整数）で表す。半径 1 の円で弧の長さが 1 となる中心角を 1 **ラジアン** とする **弧度法** では !!\(180^\circ=\pi\)!! である。半径 \(r\)、**中心角** \(\theta\) の **扇形** の **弧の長さ** は \(l=r\theta\)、面積は \(S=\dfrac{1}{2}r^2\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}rl\)。
VI: Góc mà **tia quay** OP quay từ **tia đầu** OX gọi là **góc lượng giác**, viết \(\theta+360^\circ\times n\) (\(n\) nguyên). Trong **đơn vị radian**, !!\(180^\circ=\pi\)!!. **Hình quạt** bán kính \(r\), **góc ở tâm** \(\theta\): **độ dài cung** \(l=r\theta\), diện tích \(S=\dfrac{1}{2}r^2\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}rl\).
EN: The angle through which the **moving radius** OP turns from the **initial line** OX is a **general angle**, written \(\theta+360^\circ\times n\) (\(n\) an integer). In **radian measure**, where 1 **radian** is the central angle cut by an arc of length 1 on a circle of radius 1, !!\(180^\circ=\pi\)!!. A **sector** with radius \(r\) and **central angle** \(\theta\) has **arc length** \(l=r\theta\) and area \(S=\dfrac{1}{2}r^2\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}rl\).

### 三角関数の定義
VI: Định nghĩa hàm số lượng giác
EN: Definition of the trigonometric functions

原点を中心とする半径 \(r\) の円周上の点 \(P(x,\ y)\) について、動径 OP の表す角を \(\theta\) とすると \(\sin\theta=\dfrac{y}{r}\)、\(\cos\theta=\dfrac{x}{r}\)、\(\tan\theta=\dfrac{y}{x}\)。**単位円**（\(r=1\)）では \(P(\cos\theta,\ \sin\theta)\) となる。符号は P のある **象限** で決まる。
VI: Với điểm \(P(x,\ y)\) trên đường tròn tâm gốc tọa độ, bán kính \(r\), và \(\theta\) là góc của tia OP: \(\sin\theta=\dfrac{y}{r}\), \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{x}{r}\), \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{y}{x}\). Trên **đường tròn đơn vị** (\(r=1\)): \(P(\cos\theta,\ \sin\theta)\). Dấu phụ thuộc vào **góc phần tư** chứa P.
EN: For a point \(P(x,\ y)\) on the circle of radius \(r\) centered at the origin, with \(\theta\) the angle of OP: \(\sin\theta=\dfrac{y}{r}\), \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{x}{r}\), \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{y}{x}\). On the **unit circle** (\(r=1\)), \(P(\cos\theta,\ \sin\theta)\). The signs depend on the **quadrant** containing P.

### 三角関数の相互関係
VI: Hệ thức cơ bản
EN: Basic identities

! **相互関係**：\(\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1\)、\(\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\)、\(1+\tan^2\theta=\dfrac{1}{\cos^2\theta}\)。
VI: **Hệ thức cơ bản**: \(\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1\), \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\), \(1+\tan^2\theta=\dfrac{1}{\cos^2\theta}\).
EN: **Basic identities**: \(\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1\), \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\), \(1+\tan^2\theta=\dfrac{1}{\cos^2\theta}\).
例えば \(\theta\) が第 3 象限の角で \(\tan\theta=2\) のとき、\(\cos^2\theta=\dfrac{1}{5}\) より \(\cos\theta=-\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{5}\)、\(\sin\theta=\tan\theta\cos\theta=-\dfrac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}\)。
VI: Ví dụ \(\theta\) thuộc góc phần tư thứ 3 và \(\tan\theta=2\): \(\cos^2\theta=\dfrac{1}{5}\) nên \(\cos\theta=-\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{5}\), \(\sin\theta=-\dfrac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}\).
EN: For example, if \(\theta\) is in the third quadrant and \(\tan\theta=2\), then \(\cos^2\theta=\dfrac{1}{5}\), so \(\cos\theta=-\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{5}\) and \(\sin\theta=\tan\theta\cos\theta=-\dfrac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}\).
\(\sin(-\theta)=-\sin\theta\)、\(\cos(-\theta)=\cos\theta\)、\(\sin(\theta+\pi)=-\sin\theta\)、\(\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-\theta\right)=\cos\theta\) などが成り立つ。
VI: Các tính chất: \(\sin(-\theta)=-\sin\theta\), \(\cos(-\theta)=\cos\theta\), \(\sin(\theta+\pi)=-\sin\theta\), \(\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-\theta\right)=\cos\theta\), …
EN: Also \(\sin(-\theta)=-\sin\theta\), \(\cos(-\theta)=\cos\theta\), \(\sin(\theta+\pi)=-\sin\theta\), \(\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-\theta\right)=\cos\theta\), and so on.

### 三角関数のグラフ
VI: Đồ thị hàm số lượng giác
EN: Graphs of trigonometric functions

\(y=\sin\theta\)、\(y=\cos\theta\) の **周期** は \(2\pi\)、値域は \(-1\leqq y\leqq 1\)。\(y=\tan\theta\) の周期は \(\pi\)。\(y=a\sin k\theta\) の周期は \(\dfrac{2\pi}{k}\)、**振幅** は \(|a|\)。\(y=\sin(\theta-p)\) は \(y=\sin\theta\) を \(\theta\) 軸方向に \(p\) だけ平行移動したグラフ。\(\sin\theta\) は **奇関数**（原点対称）、\(\cos\theta\) は **偶関数**（\(y\) 軸対称）である。
VI: \(y=\sin\theta\), \(y=\cos\theta\) có **chu kỳ** \(2\pi\), tập giá trị \(-1\leqq y\leqq 1\); \(y=\tan\theta\) có chu kỳ \(\pi\). \(y=a\sin k\theta\) có chu kỳ \(\dfrac{2\pi}{k}\), **biên độ** \(|a|\). \(y=\sin(\theta-p)\) là đồ thị \(y=\sin\theta\) tịnh tiến \(p\) theo trục \(\theta\). \(\sin\theta\) là **hàm lẻ** (đối xứng qua gốc), \(\cos\theta\) là **hàm chẵn** (đối xứng qua trục \(y\)).
EN: \(y=\sin\theta\) and \(y=\cos\theta\) have **period** \(2\pi\) and range \(-1\leqq y\leqq 1\); \(y=\tan\theta\) has period \(\pi\). \(y=a\sin k\theta\) has period \(\dfrac{2\pi}{k}\) and **amplitude** \(|a|\). \(y=\sin(\theta-p)\) is \(y=\sin\theta\) shifted by \(p\) along the \(\theta\)-axis. \(\sin\theta\) is an **odd function** (symmetric about the origin) and \(\cos\theta\) an **even function** (symmetric about the \(y\)-axis).

### 三角方程式・不等式
VI: Phương trình, bất phương trình lượng giác
EN: Trigonometric equations and inequalities

三角関数を含む方程式・不等式は単位円を使って解く。\(0\leqq\theta\lt 2\pi\) のとき、\(\sin\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}\) の解は \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{5}{6}\pi\)、\(\sin\theta\gt\dfrac{1}{2}\) の解は \(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\lt\theta\lt\dfrac{5}{6}\pi\)。
VI: Phương trình, bất phương trình lượng giác: dùng đường tròn đơn vị. Với \(0\leqq\theta\lt 2\pi\): \(\sin\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}\) có nghiệm \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{5}{6}\pi\); \(\sin\theta\gt\dfrac{1}{2}\) có nghiệm \(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\lt\theta\lt\dfrac{5}{6}\pi\).
EN: Solve trigonometric equations and inequalities with the unit circle. For \(0\leqq\theta\lt 2\pi\), \(\sin\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}\) gives \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{5}{6}\pi\), and \(\sin\theta\gt\dfrac{1}{2}\) gives \(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\lt\theta\lt\dfrac{5}{6}\pi\).

### 置き換えと最大・最小
VI: Đặt ẩn phụ, lớn nhất và nhỏ nhất
EN: Substitution and maximum/minimum

\(\sin\theta\) や \(\cos\theta\) の 2 次式は、\(\sin\theta=t\) などと **置き換え**、!!\(-1\leqq t\leqq 1\) に注意!!して解く。例えば \(y=\cos^2\theta+2\sin\theta=-(\sin\theta-1)^2+2\) は、\(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{2}\) で最大値 2、\(\theta=\dfrac{3}{2}\pi\) で最小値 \(-2\) をとる。
VI: Biểu thức bậc hai theo \(\sin\theta\), \(\cos\theta\): **đặt ẩn phụ** \(\sin\theta=t\), !!nhớ điều kiện \(-1\leqq t\leqq 1\)!!. Ví dụ \(y=\cos^2\theta+2\sin\theta=-(\sin\theta-1)^2+2\): lớn nhất bằng 2 tại \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{2}\), nhỏ nhất bằng \(-2\) tại \(\theta=\dfrac{3}{2}\pi\).
EN: For quadratic expressions in \(\sin\theta\) or \(\cos\theta\), **substitute** \(\sin\theta=t\) and !!remember \(-1\leqq t\leqq 1\)!!. For example, \(y=\cos^2\theta+2\sin\theta=-(\sin\theta-1)^2+2\) has maximum 2 at \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{2}\) and minimum \(-2\) at \(\theta=\dfrac{3}{2}\pi\).

### 加法定理
VI: Công thức cộng
EN: Addition formulas

**加法定理**：
VI: **Công thức cộng**:
EN: **Addition formulas**:
\[\sin(\alpha\pm\beta)=\sin\alpha\cos\beta\pm\cos\alpha\sin\beta\]
\[\cos(\alpha\pm\beta)=\cos\alpha\cos\beta\mp\sin\alpha\sin\beta\]
\[\tan(\alpha\pm\beta)=\dfrac{\tan\alpha\pm\tan\beta}{1\mp\tan\alpha\tan\beta}\]
例えば \(\sin 75^\circ=\sin(45^\circ+30^\circ)=\dfrac{\sqrt{6}+\sqrt{2}}{4}\)。
VI: Ví dụ \(\sin 75^\circ=\sin(45^\circ+30^\circ)=\dfrac{\sqrt{6}+\sqrt{2}}{4}\).
EN: For example, \(\sin 75^\circ=\sin(45^\circ+30^\circ)=\dfrac{\sqrt{6}+\sqrt{2}}{4}\).

### 2直線のなす角
VI: Góc giữa hai đường thẳng
EN: The angle between two lines

2 直線 \(y=m_1x\)、\(y=m_2x\) の **なす角** \(\theta\)（**鋭角**）は \(\tan\theta=\left|\dfrac{m_1-m_2}{1+m_1m_2}\right|\) から求められる。\(y=\sqrt{3}x\) と \(y=-\sqrt{3}x\) のなす鋭角は \(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\)。
VI: **Góc tạo bởi** hai đường thẳng \(y=m_1x\), \(y=m_2x\) (**góc nhọn**) tính từ \(\tan\theta=\left|\dfrac{m_1-m_2}{1+m_1m_2}\right|\). Hai đường \(y=\sqrt{3}x\) và \(y=-\sqrt{3}x\) tạo góc nhọn \(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\).
EN: The **angle formed** by the lines \(y=m_1x\), \(y=m_2x\) (the **acute angle** \(\theta\)) is found from \(\tan\theta=\left|\dfrac{m_1-m_2}{1+m_1m_2}\right|\). The lines \(y=\sqrt{3}x\) and \(y=-\sqrt{3}x\) form the acute angle \(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\).

### 2倍角・半角の公式
VI: Công thức góc nhân đôi, góc chia đôi
EN: Double-angle and half-angle formulas

**2 倍角の公式**：\(\sin 2\alpha=2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha\)、\(\cos 2\alpha=\cos^2\alpha-\sin^2\alpha=1-2\sin^2\alpha=2\cos^2\alpha-1\)。**半角の公式**：\(\sin^2\dfrac{\alpha}{2}=\dfrac{1-\cos\alpha}{2}\)、\(\cos^2\dfrac{\alpha}{2}=\dfrac{1+\cos\alpha}{2}\)。
VI: **Công thức góc nhân đôi**: \(\sin 2\alpha=2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha\), \(\cos 2\alpha=1-2\sin^2\alpha=2\cos^2\alpha-1\). **Công thức góc chia đôi (hạ bậc)**: \(\sin^2\dfrac{\alpha}{2}=\dfrac{1-\cos\alpha}{2}\), \(\cos^2\dfrac{\alpha}{2}=\dfrac{1+\cos\alpha}{2}\).
EN: **Double-angle formulas**: \(\sin 2\alpha=2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha\), \(\cos 2\alpha=\cos^2\alpha-\sin^2\alpha=1-2\sin^2\alpha=2\cos^2\alpha-1\). **Half-angle formulas**: \(\sin^2\dfrac{\alpha}{2}=\dfrac{1-\cos\alpha}{2}\), \(\cos^2\dfrac{\alpha}{2}=\dfrac{1+\cos\alpha}{2}\).
**積和の公式**：\(\sin\alpha\cos\beta=\dfrac{1}{2}\{\sin(\alpha+\beta)+\sin(\alpha-\beta)\}\) なども、加法定理から導かれる。
VI: **Công thức biến tích thành tổng**, ví dụ \(\sin\alpha\cos\beta=\dfrac{1}{2}\{\sin(\alpha+\beta)+\sin(\alpha-\beta)\}\), cũng suy ra từ công thức cộng.
EN: **Product-to-sum formulas** such as \(\sin\alpha\cos\beta=\dfrac{1}{2}\{\sin(\alpha+\beta)+\sin(\alpha-\beta)\}\) also follow from the addition formulas.

### 2倍角を含む方程式
VI: Phương trình có góc nhân đôi
EN: Equations with double angles

例えば \(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\lt\alpha\lt\pi\)、\(\sin\alpha=\dfrac{3}{5}\) のとき \(\cos\alpha=-\dfrac{4}{5}\)、\(\sin 2\alpha=-\dfrac{24}{25}\)、\(\cos 2\alpha=\dfrac{7}{25}\)。方程式 \(\sin 2\theta=\cos\theta\)（\(0\leqq\theta\lt 2\pi\)）は \(\cos\theta(2\sin\theta-1)=0\) より \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{\pi}{2},\ \dfrac{5}{6}\pi,\ \dfrac{3}{2}\pi\)。
VI: Ví dụ \(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\lt\alpha\lt\pi\), \(\sin\alpha=\dfrac{3}{5}\): \(\cos\alpha=-\dfrac{4}{5}\), \(\sin 2\alpha=-\dfrac{24}{25}\), \(\cos 2\alpha=\dfrac{7}{25}\). Phương trình \(\sin 2\theta=\cos\theta\) (\(0\leqq\theta\lt 2\pi\)): \(\cos\theta(2\sin\theta-1)=0\), nghiệm \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{\pi}{2},\ \dfrac{5}{6}\pi,\ \dfrac{3}{2}\pi\).
EN: For example, if \(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\lt\alpha\lt\pi\) and \(\sin\alpha=\dfrac{3}{5}\), then \(\cos\alpha=-\dfrac{4}{5}\), \(\sin 2\alpha=-\dfrac{24}{25}\), \(\cos 2\alpha=\dfrac{7}{25}\). The equation \(\sin 2\theta=\cos\theta\) (\(0\leqq\theta\lt 2\pi\)) becomes \(\cos\theta(2\sin\theta-1)=0\), so \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{\pi}{2},\ \dfrac{5}{6}\pi,\ \dfrac{3}{2}\pi\).

### 三角関数の合成
VI: Tổng hợp hàm lượng giác
EN: Combining trigonometric functions

! **三角関数の合成**：\(a\sin\theta+b\cos\theta=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\,\sin(\theta+\alpha)\)。ただし \(\cos\alpha=\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}\)、\(\sin\alpha=\dfrac{b}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}\)。
VI: **Tổng hợp hàm lượng giác**: \(a\sin\theta+b\cos\theta=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\,\sin(\theta+\alpha)\), với \(\cos\alpha=\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}\), \(\sin\alpha=\dfrac{b}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}\).
EN: **Combining**: \(a\sin\theta+b\cos\theta=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\,\sin(\theta+\alpha)\), where \(\cos\alpha=\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}\) and \(\sin\alpha=\dfrac{b}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}\).
例えば \(\sin\theta+\sqrt{3}\cos\theta=2\sin\left(\theta+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)\)。
VI: Ví dụ \(\sin\theta+\sqrt{3}\cos\theta=2\sin\left(\theta+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)\).
EN: For example, \(\sin\theta+\sqrt{3}\cos\theta=2\sin\left(\theta+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)\).

### 合成の利用
VI: Ứng dụng tổng hợp
EN: Using the combined form

\(0\leqq\theta\lt 2\pi\) のとき、\(\sin\theta+\sqrt{3}\cos\theta=1\) の解は \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{2},\ \dfrac{11}{6}\pi\)。\(y=\sin\theta+\sqrt{3}\cos\theta\) は \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{6}\) で最大値 2、\(\theta=\dfrac{7}{6}\pi\) で最小値 \(-2\) をとる。
VI: Với \(0\leqq\theta\lt 2\pi\): \(\sin\theta+\sqrt{3}\cos\theta=1\) có nghiệm \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{2},\ \dfrac{11}{6}\pi\). \(y=\sin\theta+\sqrt{3}\cos\theta\) lớn nhất bằng 2 tại \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{6}\), nhỏ nhất bằng \(-2\) tại \(\theta=\dfrac{7}{6}\pi\).
EN: For \(0\leqq\theta\lt 2\pi\), \(\sin\theta+\sqrt{3}\cos\theta=1\) has solutions \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{2},\ \dfrac{11}{6}\pi\). \(y=\sin\theta+\sqrt{3}\cos\theta\) has maximum 2 at \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{6}\) and minimum \(-2\) at \(\theta=\dfrac{7}{6}\pi\).
? 最大・最小を求めるときは、\(\theta+\alpha\) の動く範囲を先に書き出してから考える。
VI: Khi tìm lớn nhất, nhỏ nhất, hãy viết khoảng biến thiên của \(\theta+\alpha\) trước rồi mới xét.
EN: When finding the maximum and minimum, first write down the range of \(\theta+\alpha\).

## 例題
\(\theta\) が第 4 象限の角で \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{1}{3}\) のとき、\(\sin\theta\) と \(\tan\theta\) の値を求めよ。
VI: \(\theta\) thuộc góc phần tư thứ 4 và \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{1}{3}\). Tìm \(\sin\theta\) và \(\tan\theta\).
EN: \(\theta\) is in the fourth quadrant and \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{1}{3}\). Find \(\sin\theta\) and \(\tan\theta\).
解答: \(\sin^2\theta=1-\cos^2\theta=1-\dfrac{1}{9}=\dfrac{8}{9}\)。第 4 象限なので \(\sin\theta\lt 0\)、よって \(\sin\theta=-\dfrac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}\)。\(\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}=-2\sqrt{2}\) である。
VI: \(\sin^2\theta=1-\dfrac{1}{9}=\dfrac{8}{9}\). Góc phần tư thứ 4 nên \(\sin\theta\lt 0\): \(\sin\theta=-\dfrac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}\). \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}=-2\sqrt{2}\).
EN: \(\sin^2\theta=1-\dfrac{1}{9}=\dfrac{8}{9}\). In the fourth quadrant \(\sin\theta\lt 0\), so \(\sin\theta=-\dfrac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}\). \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}=-2\sqrt{2}\).

EN: \(\sin\theta=-\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\) gives \(\theta=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi,\ \dfrac{5}{3}\pi\). \(\cos\theta\gt\dfrac{1}{2}\) is the part of the unit circle with \(x\)-coordinate greater than \(\dfrac{1}{2}\): \(0\leqq\theta\lt\dfrac{\pi}{3}\) and \(\dfrac{5}{3}\pi\lt\theta\lt 2\pi\).

### 問6

VI: Với \(0\leqq\theta\lt 2\pi\), phương trình \(2\cos^2\theta+\sin\theta-1=0\) có các nghiệm theo thứ tự tăng dần: \(\pi\) chia ô A; ô B phần ô C nhân \(\pi\); ô DE phần ô F nhân \(\pi\).
EN: For \(0\leqq\theta\lt 2\pi\), the solutions of \(2\cos^2\theta+\sin\theta-1=0\), from smallest, are π/box A, box B over box C \(\pi\), and box DE over box F \(\pi\).

### 問7
\(0\leqq\theta\lt 2\pi\) のとき、関数 \(y=\sin^2\theta+\cos\theta\) の最大値は [A]/[B]（\(\theta=\) π/[C]、[D]/[E] \(\pi\) のとき）、最小値は \(-\) [F]（\(\theta=\pi\) のとき）である。

### 問8

### 問10
